## Key Papers

### Preprocessing

- Esteban, O. et al. (2019). fMRIPrep: a robust preprocessing pipeline for functional MRI. *Nature Methods*.
- Esteban, O. et al. (2020). Analysis of task-based functional MRI data preprocessed with fMRIPrep. *Nature Protocols*.

### Denoising

- Ciric, R. et al. (2017). Benchmarking of participant-level confound regression strategies for the control of motion artifact in studies of functional connectivity. *NeuroImage*.
- Powers, J.D. et al. (2012). Spurious but systematic correlations in functional connectivity MRI networks arise from subject motion. *NeuroImage*.
- Powers, J.D. et al. (2018). Ridding fMRI data of motion-related influences. *NeuroImage*.
- Wang, H-T. (2023). Continuous evaluation of denoising strategies in resting-state fMRI connectivity using fMRIPrep and Nilearn. *Preprint*.

### Quality Control

- Satterthwaite, T.D. et al. (2012). Impact of in-scanner head motion on multiple measures of functional connectivity. *NeuroImage*.

### BIDS

- Gorgolewski, K.J. et al. (2016). The brain imaging data structure, a format for organizing and describing outputs of neuroimaging experiments. *Scientific Data*.
