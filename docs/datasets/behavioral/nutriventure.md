# Nutriventure

## Overview

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Cohort Name** | Nutriventure |
| **Description** | NutriVenture is a research study that aims to test the feasibility of a personality-targeted intervention to prevent problematic eating and compulsive behaviours in adolescents. |
| **N Subjects** | 30* |
| **Timepoints** | 3 phases<br>1. Recruiting adolescents (13-17) from Sainte-Justine Hospital (clinical interviews conducted)<br>2. Trial intervention (pre-post design targeting extreme perfectionism trait), anxiety, depression and coping strategies also measured to understand impact of PreVenture on health outcomes.<br>3. Focus groups to develop feasible implementation program based on information from phases 1 and 2 |
