# CUSP

## Overview

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Cohort Name** | CUSP |
| **Description** | This cluster randomized controlled trial involves students from grade 10 enrolled in school in 2019, 2020 and 2021. Eligible students (approximately 45%) at each school will be invited to attend two PreVenture workshops. Firstly, the study will test the effectiveness of PreVenture when disseminated through a Train-the-Trainer implementation model. Consenting grade 10 students will be assessed on measures of past 12-month substance use onset as well as severity of use. Secondly, to address questions about program sustainability and scale-up, the study will apply state-of-the-art methods in implementation science to examine a novel Implementation Facilitation component to support effective and sustained delivery of PreVenture. |
| **N Subjects** | 12,150 (4050 across 27 different schools each year) |
| **Timepoints** | 3 years |
| **Data Types** | Behavioral |
